**5. How to fill the knowledge gap for the project- 4-6 Weeks**

-   **Kinetic Metallization Physics (Cold Spray vs. Thermal 3D
    Printing)-** . Cold spray uses kinetic energy---firing metal
    particles at supersonic speeds (300--1200 m/s) via a
    convergent-divergent nozzle so they plastically deform and bond upon
    impact.

**What to learn:** Critical velocity thresholds of powders (like
Titanium or Copper) and the physics of adiabatic shear instability.

-   **Micro-Porosity Formation Mechanisms-**Understanding why and where
    pores form between metal particles during impact, and how post-print
    heat treatment (like Hot Isostatic Pressing - HIP) alters grain
    boundaries to close or reshape those pores.

**What to learn:** Interparticle vs. intraparticle porosity and
temperature-time diffusion curves for metallurgical bonding

-   **Osseointegration and Biomedical Implant Mechanics-**The project
    highlights using controlled porosity for body implants to allow
    tissue growth.Need to understand how human bone cells interact with
    porous metal.

**What to learn:** Optimal pore sizes for bone ingrowth (typically
100--500 micrometers) and the biochemical degradation of biocompatible,
bioresorbable metals (like Magnesium alloys) inside human tissue fluid

-   **Machine-Specific Control (LightSPEE3D Framework)-** Understanding
    the exact parameter constraints of the physical **LightSPEE3D**
    machine hardware.

**What to learn:** How variations in carrier gas choices (Air vs.
Nitrogen or Helium), pressure limits, and robotic spray angles alter the
density of the printed part.
